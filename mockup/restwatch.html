Restwatch

A phone app that records nights and finds moments when someone may be acting out their dreams, so they can review them with a doctor. Tap through the six screens in order or jump to one.

Nothing here detects anything: the clips, scores and answers are made-up sample data. The real ranking comes from the rbdcap analysis code. The app does not diagnose; only a sleep-lab test can confirm REM sleep behavior disorder.
